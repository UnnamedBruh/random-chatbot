My Project

(author's honest note) This is a slightly dumb and impractical, but entertaining HTML5 project where you can "chat" with an assistant. However, the biggest twist is that you don't actually chat at all; you just click on hyperlinks. Think of it like a text adventure game: you're only making decisions, not talking about cheese or fantasizing about Skibidi Toilet like it's an entire fantasy multiverse.
